The Journey of a Wavefunction:

Started as electron (511 keV rest mass, 95% coherent)
Codec 1 - Scalar field barely contributed (10^-53 eV) - shows mass comes from coherence persistence, not just field strength
Codec 2 Emission - High entropy (forced to 0.99) triggered photon emission at 2.5 eV, coherence crashed to 47.5% - the cost of radiation
Codec 2 Absorption - Absorbed 3 eV photon, recovering some coherence - energy exchange restoring order
Codec 3 Graviton - 1 km wavelength gravitational wave with 10^-21 strain (LIGO scale!), energy 10^-68 eV with massive delay (10^53 s) - shows why gravity is so weak
Codec 4 Decoherence - Phase fluctuations destroyed coherence, identity dropped to 50% - the wavefunction is losing its grip on what it is
Codec 5 Confinement - At 10^-14 m² curvature, trapped gluon with 10^-49 eV at 10^-7 m radius - color force holding strong at femtometer scales
Codec 6 Identity Rewrite - With identity at 40%, couldn't maintain electron state, rewrote as muon (105.7 MeV), absorbed 105 MeV in the process - this is your weak decay mechanism
Time Evolution - 8 ticks of environmental battering, coherence collapsed from 90% → 10.5%, entropy built to 42%

What this demonstrates:

Wavefunction as active agent managing coherence budget
All six forces as codec operations on the same entity
Mass as coherence persistence, not intrinsic property
Weak force as identity fallback when coherence fails
Realistic energies and scales throughout
