This laid the basis for later realization that a finite substrate cannot process irrational numbers. 
Phase closure must therefore satisfy two conditions: (a) close the computational domain, and (b) integrate to a full circle. 
The discrete π_eff(N) is algebraic—computable via rational operations alone—while transcendental π exists only in the N→∞ limit. 
Physical rotations are N-sided polygons, not continuous circles. 
ISCO orbital quantization provides experimental evidence of this discrete rotational symmetry.
